These focus on the landing page (marketing, features, how it works) and app (dashboard, account, docs). Primary keywords are core terms; long-tail are more specific phrases. Search volume and competition estimates are based on general Web3/Solana market trends (e.g., "Solana" has high search interest due to ecosystem growth, while niche terms like "token delegation subscriptions" have lower volume). Intent reflects user goals: commercial (purchasing/signing up), informational (learning), transactional (managing account), navigational (finding docs). Target audience: developers (technical integration), SaaS providers (business adoption), content creators (monetization).
