## What is Tributary?

Tributary enables **automated USDC subscriptions** on Solana via token delegation. Think Netflix billing, but decentralized - users approve once, then monthly renewals happen seamlessly without any manual intervention.

**🔥 Key Benefits for Your Project:**

- **Predictable Revenue**: Transform one-time payments into recurring monthly income
- **Zero Customer Friction**: Users sign up once, auto-renew forever (until they choose to stop)
- **Web2 UX on Web3**: Familiar subscription experience your users already understand
- **Ultra-Low Fees**: Under $0.01 per transaction (vs 2-3% traditional payment processors)
- **Non-Custodial**: Users maintain full control of their funds
