# Tributary Protocol: Go-to-Market Strategy

## 1. Executive Summary

### Vision

Position Tributary as the foundational infrastructure for automated recurring payments on Solana, capturing 15% of the Web3 payments market ($7.5B) within 36 months through a dual-sided strategy targeting developers/providers and end-users.

### Timeline

- **Phase 1 (Months 1-6):** Foundation & Early Adoption
- **Phase 2 (Months 7-18):** Growth & Ecosystem Expansion
- **Phase 3 (Months 19-36):** Scale & Market Leadership

### Key Objectives

1. Onboard 50+ providers in first 12 months
2. Process $10M in payment volume by Month 12
3. Achieve 100,000+ active subscriptions by Month 18
4. Establish technical standard for Solana recurring payments
5. Generate $150K monthly protocol revenue by Month 24

### Success Metrics

- Provider Count & Quality
- Total Value Locked (TVL) in active subscriptions
- Transaction Volume & Growth Rate
- Developer SDK downloads & implementations
- User retention & subscription renewal rates
- Protocol revenue & fee collection

## 3. Product-Market Fit

### Pain Points Addressed

#### For Businesses

- **Problem:** 67% cart abandonment due to manual approvals
- **Solution:** One-time delegation = Web2-like checkout
- **Impact:** 2-3x conversion improvement

- **Problem:** Unpredictable revenue, manual invoicing
- **Solution:** Automated payment execution, guaranteed timing
- **Impact:** Improved cash flow forecasting

- **Problem:** Building payment infrastructure = 3-6 months dev time
- **Solution:** SDK integration in days, not months
- **Impact:** Faster time-to-market, lower development costs

#### For Users

- **Problem:** Repetitive wallet approvals (every payment)
- **Solution:** Sign once, automated execution
- **Impact:** Frictionless experience

- **Problem:** Fund lock-up in smart contracts
- **Solution:** Non-custodial delegation, funds stay in wallet
- **Impact:** Capital efficiency, no custody risk

- **Problem:** Lack of control over subscriptions
- **Solution:** Revocable anytime, transparent on-chain
- **Impact:** Trust, flexibility

- ✅ Team: 10+ years combined Web3 experience

## 8. Revenue & Monetization

### Revenue Model

#### Protocol Fees (Primary Revenue)

**Standard Fee Structure:**

- **Base Rate:** 1% of payment volume
- **Applied to:** All successful payment executions
- **Collection:** Automatic via smart contract
- **Distribution:** 100% to protocol treasury (Year 1-2)

**Volume-Based Discounts:**

| Monthly Volume | Protocol Fee | Effective Annual Fee |
| -------------- | ------------ | -------------------- |
| $0 - $1M       | 1.0%         | 1.0%                 |
| $1M - $10M     | 0.75%        | 0.75%                |
| $10M - $50M    | 0.5%         | 0.5%                 |
| $50M+          | 0.25%        | 0.25%                |

**Fee Waivers:**

- Design partners: 3 months free
- Strategic partners: Negotiated rates
- Ecosystem grants: Case-by-case
- Non-profit/education: 50% discount

#### Provider Revenue Model (Informational)

While Tributary doesn't directly earn from provider fees, healthy provider economics are critical:

**Provider Fee Ranges:**

- Typical: 2-3% of payment volume
- Premium services: 3-5%
- Enterprise: Custom pricing
- White-label: 5-10%

**Provider Revenue Streams:**

- Transaction fees (primary)
- SaaS subscriptions
- Premium features
- Analytics/reporting
- Custom integrations
- Consulting services
