# Platforms to target with Solana/USDC subscriptions

## Membership/Subscription Platforms

Ko-fi - Very similar to Buy Me a Coffee, offers one-time donations and memberships with lower fees
Memberful - Integrates with your existing website, owned by Patreon
Substack - Focused on newsletters with paid subscriptions
Ghost - Open-source platform for paid newsletters and memberships
Gumroad - Sell digital products and offer memberships

## Tip/Donation Focused

Tipeee - Popular in Europe, similar to Patreon
Liberapay - Non-profit, recurrent donations platform
Thanks.io - Simple tip jar for creators

## Video/Streaming Specific

YouTube Memberships - Channel memberships for YouTube creators
Twitch Subscriptions - For streamers on Twitch
Facebook Stars/Subscriptions - For Facebook content creators

## Multi-platform Options

Kickstarter/Indiegogo - Project-based crowdfunding (not recurring)
OnlyFans - Subscription platform (not just adult content despite reputation)
Fanhouse - Creator subscriptions, especially for influencers

## Community-Focused

Circle - Community platform with payment features
Mighty Networks - Paid communities and courses
