# Solana Foundation Grant Application Draft

## Project Overview

Tributary: Automated recurring USDC payments on Solana via delegation. Enables Web2 subscriptions for Web3 indie projects.

## Problem Solved

Indie Solana projects struggle with monetization – high fees, manual payments, on-chain friction. Tributary abstracts this for SaaS revenue.

## Solution

- Smart contracts: Policies, gateways, delegates.
- SDK: Easy integration for devs.
- Non-custodial: Users control funds, approve once.

## Impact

- 15+ providers in 6 months, $50K+ TPV.
- Boosts ecosystem adoption, especially DePIN/SocialFi.

## Grant Request

$10K for marketing/outreach. In return: Free integrations for grant recipients, co-promotion.

## Milestones

- Month 1: 5 integrations.
- Month 3: 10 providers.
- Month 6: 500 community members.

Apply at <https://solana.com/solutions/request-for-startups>. Offer free Tributary setup to winners.
