# Tributary Program Audit — 2026-07-09

**Scope:** Full program audit of `programs/tributary/` (Rust/Anchor 0.31.0).
**Outcome:** No Critical/High fund-drain vectors found. Three findings filed
(1 Medium, 2 Low) — all resolved in epic `tributary-3nhr`.

### M-01 — Milestone RELEASE_RECIPIENT release-condition deadlocked

A Milestone policy with `release_condition & RELEASE_RECIPIENT` (bit 3) could
never execute. `validate_policy_execution` required `caller == recipient`, but
`execute_payment` rejected `fee_payer == recipient` for Milestone policies.
No signer could satisfy both → escrow permanently locked.

**Fix:** Extracted `recipient_can_trigger()` predicate into `shared::schedule`;
extended the `execute_payment` recipient allow-list to include Milestone when
the `RELEASE_RECIPIENT` bit is set.

### L-01 — `emergency_pause` kill-switch non-functional

`ProgramConfig.emergency_pause` was enforced at 20 execute sites but hardcoded
to `false` at `initialize` with no on-chain setter. The kill-switch was read-only
dead code — a genuine emergency could not be halted without a program upgrade.

**Fix:** Added admin-gated `set_emergency_pause(paused: bool)` instruction,
mirroring `ChangeProgramAuthority`.

### L-03 — `create_payment_policy` accepts zero-address recipient

`create_payment_policy` accepted `recipient == Pubkey::default()` while
`create_composable_policy` rejected it — asymmetric validation.

**Fix:** Added `Pubkey::default()` constraint to the `recipient` account.
